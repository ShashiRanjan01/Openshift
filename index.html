Knowledge check

Scenario: the Pod shows Running, but users receive 503 Service Unavailable. What is a strong first troubleshooting step?

Readiness vs. liveness vs. startup

  • Readiness: determines whether a Pod should receive Service traffic.
  • Liveness: repeated failures can cause the container to be restarted.
  • Startup: gives a slow-starting application time to initialize before liveness checks take effect.
